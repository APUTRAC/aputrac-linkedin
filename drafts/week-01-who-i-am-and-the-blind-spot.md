For ten years I led fuel efficiency work at IATA. The biggest gap I found wasn't in the cockpit, the flight plan, or the engines you'd expect.

It was the one engine nobody watches.

Every engine on an aircraft is managed in real time except one: the APU.

On a gate, on a remote stand, overnight in maintenance, an APU can be running and burning fuel while no one who could switch it off knows. Most airlines look at the data after landing. By then, the fuel is gone and the reason is hard to find.
